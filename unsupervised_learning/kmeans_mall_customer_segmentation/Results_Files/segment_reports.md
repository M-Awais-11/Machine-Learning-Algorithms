# Mall Customer Segmentation Report

Method: K-Means (k=6) on scaled Age, Income, Spending Score.

## Segment 0: Older, Mid-income, moderate-spending
- Size: 45 customers
- Avg age: 56.3 years
- Avg income: 54.3k$
- Avg spending score: 49.1
- Female ratio: 60%
- **Recommended action:** Cross-sell and upsell, reward-based programmes to nudge spending upward. Channel: email/SMS, comfort and value-focused messaging.

## Segment 1: Young, Mid-income, moderate-spending
